Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the STL to Ladder mobile application.

Information We Do Not Collect

STL to Ladder does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from users. The application does not require user registration, login, or the submission of personal information.

Application Data

STL to Ladder is designed to convert Siemens Statement List (STL) code into Ladder Logic representations. The STL code and other information entered or processed by the user are handled locally on the user's device.

User-provided code, conversion results, and other application inputs are not uploaded to our servers or transmitted to third-party services.

Use of Data

Information entered into the application is used only to provide the conversion and related functionality requested by the user. We do not use user-provided content for tracking, profiling, advertising, or other purposes.

Third-Party Services

STL to Ladder does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-sharing services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user information with third parties. Since the application does not collect or transmit personal information or user-provided code, there is no such data stored on our servers for sharing.

Children's Privacy

STL to Ladder does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. The application does not require users to provide personal information or create an account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
